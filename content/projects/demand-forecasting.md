## Summary
Used MDA (major domestic appliance) market indicators to improve demand forecasting accuracy and connect the outputs to business insight.

## Pipeline
- SARIMAX (R): forecast · caret · tseries · lmtest
- R Package: devtools · usethis · roxygen2 · testthat

## Outcome
- Achieved average MAPE of 0.9 to 7.5 percent and MASE of 0.3 to 0.8
- Kept AICc within plus or minus 5 despite heavier computation, balancing accuracy and stability
